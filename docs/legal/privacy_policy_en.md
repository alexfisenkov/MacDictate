# PRIVACY POLICY
For MacDictate Software and MacDictate.pro website

This Policy regulates the collection, use, and protection of personal and technical data of Users of the "MacDictate" application and visitors of the MacDictate.pro website.

## 1. INTRODUCTION AND ARCHITECTURE (IMPORTANT!)
**1.1. Local Speech and Text Processing:** The main principle of MacDictate is ABSOLUTE PRIVACY. The application uses the offline neural network OpenAI Whisper for speech recognition and may use a local Qwen model for improving recognized text.
Your voice, audio recordings, background noise, transcribed text, and text passed to the improvement feature are processed EXCLUSIVELY on your device's processor (locally on macOS). **Not a single byte of dictation audio or text is sent to our servers or third-party servers.**

## 2. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT AND WHY
To ensure the operation of the license, the trial period mechanism, and the payment process, we collect the minimum necessary set of data:

**2.1. Strictly necessary technical data (Non-personally identifiable):**
- Hardware UUID / Machine ID of your macOS device. *Purpose: tracking the 60 free days and authorizing the license.*

**2.2. Data provided voluntarily during purchase:**
- Email address. *Purpose: sending payment receipts, sending the license key (if necessary), and notifications about subscription expiration.*
- Payment data (Card number). **Important:** we do not collect, process, or store bank card numbers! Payment takes place strictly within the secure environment of the acquiring bank / payment gateway (e.g., Stripe, Tinkoff). We only receive the status: "Payment was successful".

## 3. HOW WE PROTECT YOUR DATA
3.1. The bundle `Hardware ID` + `Email` is stored on rented secure servers without direct public access.
3.2. We do not transfer your Email to third-party spam services or advertising networks.
3.3. The only contractor receiving data (Email and Amount) is the payment provider solely for the purpose of fulfilling legal requirements for issuing a receipt.

## 4. COOKIES
4.1. The MacDictate.pro website may use short cookies to save the selected language and maintain the payment session. The user has the right to disable cookies in the browser.

## 5. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR Compliance)
5.1. The user has the right at any time to request the deletion of their Email and device ID from the license database by contacting us through the MacDictate.pro website.
5.2. Deleting this data will result in the cancellation of the active license, as the server will no longer be able to authorize your device.
